In this session, you will learn how to use the Fulfillment Configuration Utility and how to access terms of use and policies for an individual patron and specific item from the utility. To open the utility, you'll go to Fulfillment, Advanced Tools, Loans, Fulfillment Configuration Utility. You can either look up a specific patron by entering their name or primary identifier, which is what we'll do in this case, since we know who the patron is. You can also select from a list. Repeat this process for the item barcode. Click OK once you've entered both. Here you can see the fulfillment unit name and rule, as well as the terms of use for the item. Also listed is the due date if the item were to be loaned now. Click on the associated fulfillment attribute name to view and edit them. For more information about fulfillment units, please watch the Fulfillment Units tutorial in the Alma Essentials Training Sessions in the Knowledge Center.
